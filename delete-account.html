Delete Your Account

You can delete your Electrician Pro X account and all associated data directly from within the app, or by contacting us.

Delete In-App

  1. Open the Electrician Pro X app on your device
  2. Tap the menu icon (☰) in the top-right corner of the header
  3. Select Settings from the menu
  4. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account
  5. Confirm your decision when prompted — deletion is immediate

What Gets Deleted

  • Account profile and login credentials
  • All jobs, estimates, and invoices
  • Photos and documents attached to jobs
  • Time tracking records and analytics data
  • Team associations and job invitations

This action is permanent and cannot be undone. Export any data you need before deleting your account.

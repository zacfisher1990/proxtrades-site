Description of Service

HVAC Pro X is a business application designed for heating, ventilation, and air conditioning (HVAC) contractors and technicians. The App provides tools for:

  • Job management, scheduling, and Google Calendar sync
  • Creating and sending estimates, including AI-assisted estimates
  • Creating and sending invoices
  • Online invoice payments via Stripe
  • Leads, clients, and a price book of services and materials
  • Crew management, dispatch, job messages, and time tracking
  • Expenses, receipt scanning, mileage, and business analytics
  • QuickBooks Online sync of invoices, clients, payments, and expenses
  • Tune-up checklists and service reports
  • HVAC field tools and references, including refrigerant pressure-temperature charts, charge checks, airflow, combustion, and electrical diagnostics

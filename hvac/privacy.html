Privacy Policy

HVAC Pro X

Last Updated: November 1, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Pro X Trades LLC ("we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, discloses, and safeguards your information when you use HVAC Pro X, our mobile and web application for heating, ventilation, and air conditioning (HVAC) contractors (the "App"), including the web app at app.hvac.proxtrades.com and the estimate, invoice, and quote request pages it creates. Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address
  • Name (optional)
  • Password (stored by our authentication provider, never in readable form)

If you choose to sign in with Apple, we receive your Apple ID credentials and any information you authorize Apple to share (such as your name and email address). Apple may provide us with a private relay email address if you choose to hide your email.

Business Profile

You may add your company name, logo, phone number, business email, business address, contractor license number, and document preferences (font and brand color). These details appear on the estimates and invoices you send.

Job and Business Data

When you use the App to manage your HVAC business, you may enter:

  • Job names, descriptions, notes, and to-do lists
  • Client names and contact information (including phone numbers, email addresses, and addresses)
  • Job locations and addresses
  • Scheduled dates and times
  • Estimates, invoices, line items, prices, discounts, and payments you record
  • Job photos and photo markups
  • Tune-up and maintenance reports, including checklist results, equipment readings, and technician notes
  • Customer signatures collected on a job or an estimate
  • Expenses, receipt photos, and mileage
  • Your price book of services and materials
  • Leads, including details and photos submitted through your quote request form

This information is stored to provide you with the App's job management, estimating, invoicing, scheduling, and reporting features.

Crew Information

If you invite crew members or managers, we store their email addresses, names, the jobs assigned to them, their job status updates, messages and photos posted on a job, and their time entries. If you set pay rates, we store those rates; only you, and managers you allow, can see them.

Location Information

The App uses your device's location only in these cases:

  • Tap to Pay: when you take an in-person card payment, Stripe requires your location to help prevent fraud.
  • Clock-in locations: if the business you work for turns on clock-in location recording, your location is saved at the moment you clock in or out of a job. It is not tracked at any other time. You are told when this setting is on, and you can decline the permission and still clock in.

We do not collect location in the background.

Contacts

If you choose to import a client from your phone's contacts, the App reads only the contact you select. We do not upload your address book.

Google Calendar Data

If you choose to connect your Google Calendar account, we access your Google Calendar solely to create, update, and delete calendar events for your jobs. Specifically, we:

  • Create calendar events when you schedule a job in the App
  • Update those events when job details (date, time, title, or notes) change
  • Delete events when you remove a job or disconnect Google Calendar

We do not read, store, or share any existing events from your Google Calendar. We only write events that originate from HVAC Pro X. Your Google Calendar access token is stored securely in your account and is never shared with third parties. You can revoke this access at any time from within the App or directly through your Google Account settings.

HVAC Pro X's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use Google user data to develop, improve, or train artificial intelligence or machine learning models, we do not sell it, and we do not use it for advertising.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, we store a push notification token for your device so we can tell you about estimate responses, payments, new leads, crew activity, job messages, and job reminders.

Automatically Collected Information

We may automatically collect certain information when you use the App, including:

  • Device type and operating system
  • Which features you use and how often (for example, which field tools you open)
  • Crash reports and performance data

The App also includes an optional diagnostics recorder in Settings. It is off unless you turn it on, the log stays on your device, and it is only sent to us if you choose to share it.

Information About Your Clients

When your client opens an estimate or invoice link, we record when it was viewed and how many times, so you can see that it was opened. When someone submits your quote request form, we keep a one-way hash of their IP address for a short time to block spam.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Provide job management, estimating, invoicing, scheduling, crew, and time tracking features
  • Send estimates, invoices, receipts, and invitations to the people you choose, on your behalf
  • Sync your scheduled jobs to Google Calendar when you choose to connect it
  • Generate AI estimate suggestions and read receipt photos when you use those features
  • Process subscription payments and your clients' invoice payments
  • Send you notifications and important updates about the App or your account
  • Improve and fix the App
  • Respond to customer support requests

How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information in the following situations:

Service Providers

We use third-party services to operate the App:

  • Firebase (Google): For authentication, database storage, file storage, and server functions
  • Google Gemini (Google): When you use AI Estimate Assist or receipt scanning, the job description you write, or the receipt photo you take, is sent to Google's Gemini API to generate the result. Your Google Calendar data is never sent to it.
  • Google Calendar API: To create and manage calendar events when you connect Google Calendar. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Postmark and Resend: For sending emails, such as estimates, invoices, receipts, invitations, and account notices
  • Stripe: For processing invoice payments from your clients, including Tap to Pay
  • RevenueCat: For managing subscriptions
  • Apple and Google Play: For processing in-app subscriptions, and Sign in with Apple
  • Intuit QuickBooks: If you connect QuickBooks Online, the invoices, payments, and expenses you choose to sync are sent to your QuickBooks account
  • Expo: For delivering push notifications to your device

People You Work With

When you send an estimate or invoice, the recipient sees its contents and your business details. When you assign a job to a crew member, they see that job's details, address, client contact information, notes, photos, and messages. Crew members do not see your prices, invoices, expenses, or other financial information.

Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law, court order, or governmental authority, or when we believe disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, your safety, or the safety of others.

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored using Firebase, a Google Cloud service, with encryption in transit and at rest. We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data Retention

We retain your account information and business data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you services. If you delete your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal information within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it for legal purposes (for example, payment records).

Your Rights and Choices

Access and Update

You can access and update your account and business information within the App at any time.

Delete Your Account

You can delete your account and its data from within the App (Settings, then Delete Account), or by contacting us at support@proxtrades.com. See how to delete your account.

Permissions

You can turn off location, contacts, camera, photo, and notification access at any time in your device settings. Some features will not work without them.

Email Communications

You can opt out of promotional emails by following the unsubscribe instructions in those emails. You will still receive transactional emails related to your account and services.

Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will delete it immediately.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us at:

Email: support@proxtrades.com

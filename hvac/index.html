Everything an HVAC business runs on

Built for how HVAC techs work, not adapted from a generic home-services tool.

Estimates with AI assist

Describe the job in plain English and get a starting estimate with equipment, line sets, ductwork and labor hours, priced from your own price book first. Send it as a link the client can accept or sign online.

Invoices and online payments

Send invoices clients can pay by card or bank transfer through Stripe. Record cash and check payments, take deposits, and send receipts. We add no fee on top of Stripe's.

Jobs and scheduling

Track every job from the first call to the final sign-off, with photos, notes, to-dos and a customer signature. Put jobs on the calendar and sync them to Google Calendar.

Crew dispatch and time cards

Invite your crew, assign jobs on a dispatch board, message them on the job, and review time cards with overtime worked out before you export hours for payroll.

Leads and your price book

Collect leads from your website, Google, Thumbtack and more. Save your flat-rate services and your materials with markup, and add them to estimates in a tap.

HVAC field tools

PT charts, superheat and subcooling, duct sizing, static pressure, psychrometrics, combustion analysis, compressor and capacitor checks, and more. Free on every plan.

Tune-up reports

Work through a cooling or furnace maintenance checklist, record readings, flag what needs attention, and save the report to the job for your records and your customer.

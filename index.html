Our Mission

Software That Speaks Your Language.

Jobber, Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan — built for "home services." That means one tool crammed with generic workflows for every trade. Electricians, plumbers, and HVAC techs all get shoehorned into the same mold, one that was never designed for any of them specifically.

At Pro X Trades LLC, we take a different approach. We build a dedicated app for each trade — one that understands how that trade works, what paperwork they produce, what tools they need on the job site, and how their business actually runs.

That's why we have three apps, not one: Electrician Pro X for electrical contractors, Plumber Pro X for plumbers, and HVAC Pro X for heating and cooling contractors. Each new app we build will bring that same level of craft to another trade.

How We Build

Our Approach.

The principles that guide every app we build.

🎯

Trade-Specific

We build one app per trade, not one app for every trade. Each product is purpose-built for the workflows, documents, and field tools that trade actually uses.

📱

Mobile-First

Tradespeople work in the field. Every app we build is designed for a phone first — fast, reliable, and useful whether you're in the office or on the job site.

⚡

Office & Field in One

Business management tools and trade-specific field tools — all in the same app. No switching between a CRM and a separate reference tool.

🆓

Free to Start

Every app we build includes a free plan. Tradespeople shouldn't have to pay just to try software — get started free, upgrade when it earns it.

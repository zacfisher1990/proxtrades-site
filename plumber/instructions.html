Getting started

Create your account

Open the web app at app.plumber.proxtrades.com (or the phone app), tap Sign Up, and enter your name, email address, and a password. If your email looks mistyped, the app asks Did you mean…? so you don't lock yourself out with a typo.

Find your way around

The app opens on Estimates. On your phone, the bar at the bottom has Estimates, Invoices, Jobs, Schedule, and More. Tap More for everything else: Business Profile, Inbox, Clients, Crew, Price Book, Time Tracking, Field Tools, Expenses, Analytics, and Settings. On the web app these are in the left sidebar.

Set up your business profile

Tap your profile picture in the top-right corner (or More → Business Profile). Fill in your company name, name, logo, phone, business email, address, and contractor license number. Everything saves as you go and appears on every estimate and invoice. In the Document Appearance card you can pick a font and a brand color for your documents.

The free plan includes 3 jobs, 3 estimates, 3 invoices, and 3 AI estimates, so you can try everything before you subscribe.

Estimates

Create an estimate

Tap Estimates, then the + button (Add Estimate on the web app). Tap Add line item for a description, quantity, and unit price, or Add from price book to add a saved service or material. Use + Add discount to take money off. In Terms, turn on Require Deposit to collect a deposit, or Include Contract to have the client e-sign before accepting.

AI Estimate Assist

Tap AI Estimate Assist and describe the job in plain English, for example "replace a 50 gallon gas water heater with a new expansion tank" or "add a full bathroom in the finished basement". You get labor hours, pipe and fittings, and fixtures to review. Pipe is counted in sticks and coils, not by the foot, and anything you've saved in your Price Book is priced at your own cost first. Choose how the client sees it: Scope of work (one price with a description of the work), Labor + materials, or Itemized. Set your markup and add it to the estimate. Always check the quantities and prices before you send.

Send it and get a response

Open the estimate and tap Send: Text a link, Email it, Copy link, or Share the PDF. When the client opens it you'll see Viewed and get a notification. They can Accept, Request changes, or Decline online, and you're notified either way.

Turn it into an invoice

Open an accepted estimate and tap Create Invoice; the line items carry over. If the client pays a required deposit online, a deposit invoice is created for you.

Jobs

Create a job

Tap Jobs, then +. Add a title, pick a client, and add the location and notes (gate codes, where the shutoff is, and so on). Set the status to Scheduled, In Progress, or Completed.

Next stop and jobs to invoice

When you have a job today, the Next stop card at the top of Jobs lets you Navigate, text the client that you're On my way, or Start to clock in. Completed jobs without an invoice show under Needs attention; tap Invoice to create one from the job.

Photos, to-dos, and money

Attach before-and-after photos (up to 30 per job) and mark them up. Build a checklist on the To-Do tab. In the Financial section, link estimates and invoices, add job expenses, and log mileage to see your profit on the job.

Before finishing and customer sign-off

The Before Finishing section can require photos, a finished checklist, and the customer's signature before a job is marked done. Tap Get customer signature and hand the phone over. The signature shows as Work completed and accepted on the job's invoices.

Invoices

Tap Invoices, then +, or start from an accepted estimate or a completed job. Pick Payment Terms (Due on Receipt, Net 15, 30, 45, or 60) and the due date is set for you. Tap Send to text, email, copy, or share a view-and-pay link.

Once Stripe is connected, clients can pay by card or bank transfer from the link, and the payment is recorded automatically. For cash or checks, tap Record payment; partial payments are supported. After a payment, tap Send → Receipt to send a receipt. The Outstanding row at the top shows what you're owed, and overdue invoices show under Needs attention with a Remind button.

Schedule

Tap Schedule. Tap a day, then + to create a new job or put an existing one on that day. Tap the time on a job card to set start and end times; jobs with a start time get a reminder about an hour before. Each card has quick buttons to call, text, get directions, and clock in.

To sync with Google Calendar, go to Settings → Integrations → Google Calendar → Connect, or tap the Google Calendar icon on the calendar. Jobs are then added to your Google Calendar when you schedule them and updated or removed when they change.

Payments

Connect Stripe

Open Business Profile and scroll to Payment Methods. Tap Connect Stripe Account and follow the short setup to verify your business and link your bank account. Client payments go straight to your Stripe account.

Fees

We add no fee. You pay only Stripe's standard fees: 2.9% + $0.30 per card payment and 0.8% (capped at $5) per bank transfer. To have clients cover the fee, turn on Pass card processing fee to client in Payment Methods (check that surcharging is allowed in your state).

Tap to Pay (Android)

In the Android app, open a Pending or Partial invoice and tap Tap to Pay. The client taps their card or phone on yours and the payment is recorded. Stripe charges 2.7% + 15¢ per Tap to Pay payment. The first time, the app asks for location access, which Stripe requires.

Other payment methods and QuickBooks

Under Other ways to get paid, add Venmo, Zelle, check, and so on so clients see them on invoices. To sync with QuickBooks Online, go to Settings → Integrations → QuickBooks Online and connect. You choose what to sync: use Sync to QuickBooks on an invoice, a job, or the Expenses screen.

Leads

Open More → Inbox → Leads. Add phone-call and referral leads with +. Open a lead to call, text, or email them, then tap Convert to Estimate or Schedule job to start with their details filled in. New clients are added when you save. Leads don't count toward the free plan's limits.

Your website request form

Go to Settings → Integrations → Lead Sources. You get a ready-made Request a quote page with your business name, logo, and color. Copy the link for a "Get a quote" button, your Google Business Profile, or a QR code, or copy the embed code into your website builder. Requests arrive on your Leads tab with a phone notification.

Other lead sources

For Thumbtack, Angi, Google Ads lead forms, Facebook, and others, use Your Lead URL on the same screen, usually through Zapier or Make. The screen walks you through it and shows Lead received when your first test arrives. Keep your Lead URL private.

Clients

Open More → Clients and tap +. Only the name is required. On your phone you can import a client from your contacts. Tap a client to see their details and recent jobs and invoices, with buttons to call, text, email, and edit. You can also edit a client from inside an estimate, invoice, or job without leaving the form.

Crew

The Crew plan lets you run a team. Crew members and managers join free; only you need the subscription.

Invite and assign

Open More → Crew and tap + to invite someone by email. They accept from their Inbox. To give them work, tap Assign Job on their card, or Assign Crew on the job, and pick the days (times are optional).

Dispatch board

The Dispatch tab shows everyone's week or day. Tap an unassigned job, then a day in someone's row, to place it. Tap a job on the board to change its date or time, reassign it, or remove it. On the web app you can drag and drop.

In the field

Crew tap On My Way, Start Job (clocks in), and Finish Job (clocks out), and you're notified at each step. Each job with crew has a Messages tab for text and photos. Crew don't see your prices, invoices, or expenses.

Time cards and payroll

Tap Time Cards on a crew member's card to review hours, fix or add entries, set a pay rate, and Approve week. Choose the overtime rule in Crew Settings (weekly over 40, daily over 8, Colorado, or California). Tap Hours to export a spreadsheet for payroll. Pay figures are estimates, not payroll advice.

Reminders, locations, and managers

In Crew Settings, choose morning-of job lists and hour-before reminders, set what every job needs before it's finished, and optionally turn on Record clock-in location (saved only when someone clocks in or out). Under Managers, invite an office manager or partner to dispatch and run time cards for you.

Inbox

More → Inbox has two tabs. Alerts shows crew and job invitations, estimate responses, invoice payments and overdue notices, when a client views a document, new leads, and job messages. Leads is your leads list. Tap an alert to open what it's about.

Price Book

More → Price Book has two tabs. On Services, add the work you charge a flat price for, like "Replace tank water heater", with a description your client sees on the line. Below your services is a list of common plumbing services to start from; tap one and set your price. On Materials, add parts with your unit cost and markup, and the app shows what the client pays.

On an estimate or invoice, tap Add from price book. When you type a new line, tick Save to price book to keep it for next time. AI Estimate Assist uses your saved material costs first, so the more you save, the closer its prices get to yours.

Time tracking

Open a job and tap Clock In (or Start on Next stop, or the play button on a calendar card). A clock icon in the top bar shows you're on the clock. Tap Clock Out when you're done. More → Time Tracking shows your time cards by week; open a week to Share PDF or Email it. Forgot to clock in? Tap + to add an entry by hand.

Field tools

More → Field Tools. Free on every plan.

  • Rolling Offset for travel, cut length, and fitting takeoff on 22½°, 45°, and 60° offsets
  • Drain Sizing from drainage fixture units, with slope
  • Gas Pipe Sizing by BTU load and longest run, for natural gas and propane
  • Water Heater Sizing for tank first-hour rating and tankless flow and temperature rise
  • Pipe Reference for dimensions, volume, water weight, and pressure in feet of head for copper, PEX, CPVC, and steel pipe
  • Unit Conversion including pressure and flow rate

Field tools are references. Always confirm against the code adopted in your area and your local inspector.

Expenses

More → Expenses → +. Enter the amount, description, category, date, and vendor, link it to a job to track that job's real cost, and attach a receipt photo. On the Solo and Crew plans, tap Scan receipt and the app fills in the amount, vendor, and date for you. Connected to QuickBooks? Tap Sync to send expenses over.

Analytics

More → Analytics shows your jobs, estimates, and invoices, revenue by when payments came in, hours, expenses, mileage, and net profit for the week, month, year, or all time. Under Tax report, pick a year and tap Export Tax Report for a spreadsheet you can hand to your tax preparer.

Settings

More → Settings (bottom of the sidebar on the web app). Here you can change your password, see your plan and what you've used, upgrade or Manage your subscription, switch dark mode, and connect QuickBooks, Google Calendar, and Lead Sources. Under Support you'll find this guide, Contact Support, and Record diagnostics: if the app ever stops responding to taps, turn it on, and the next time it happens, reopen the app and tap Share diagnostics to send us the log. Delete Account is under Danger Zone; see deleting your account.

Frequently asked questions

What does the free plan include?

3 jobs, 3 estimates, 3 invoices, and 3 AI estimates, plus all field tools. To create a fourth job, estimate, or invoice you'll need a subscription. There's no free trial of the paid plans.

How much are the paid plans?

Solo is $9.99 a month or $99.99 a year, and removes the free limits and adds receipt scanning. Crew is $12.99 a month or $129.99 a year, and adds your crew, dispatch, and time cards.

Do my crew members need a subscription?

No. Crew members and managers join and work free. Only the account that invites and assigns needs the Crew plan.

Does Plumber Pro X take a cut of my payments?

No. You pay only Stripe's own processing fees, and the money goes straight to your Stripe account.

Can I use it offline?

Yes. You can create jobs, estimates, and invoices without a connection and they sync when you're back online. Sending to a client needs a connection.

How do I cancel?

Settings → Manage on your subscription. On iPhone this opens App Store subscriptions, on Android Google Play, and on the web the billing page. You keep access until the end of the period you've paid for.

Is my data secure?

Your data is stored in Google's Firebase with encryption in transit and at rest. We don't sell your data. See the Privacy Policy.

Email us at support@proxtrades.com and we'll get back to you.

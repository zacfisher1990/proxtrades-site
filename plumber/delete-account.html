Delete Your Account

Plumber Pro X

You can delete your Plumber Pro X account and the data that goes with it from inside the app, or by contacting us.

Delete in the app

  1. Open Plumber Pro X on your phone, or the web app at app.plumber.proxtrades.com, and sign in.
  2. Tap More, then Settings (on the web app, Settings is at the bottom of the left sidebar).
  3. Scroll to Danger Zone at the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm when prompted. Deletion starts right away.

What gets deleted

  • Your account, profile, and login
  • Your business profile and logo
  • Jobs, estimates, invoices, clients, and leads
  • Photos, signatures, and documents attached to jobs
  • Your price book, expenses, receipts, and mileage
  • Time tracking records
  • Your crew, invitations, and job assignments
  • Your Google Calendar and QuickBooks connections

This is permanent. Deleted data can't be recovered. Export anything you need first, such as invoices, time cards, or the tax report from Analytics.

What we keep

Records we are required to keep by law, such as payment records, may be retained for as long as the law requires. Payments your clients made through Stripe stay in your Stripe account, which you manage with Stripe. If you have a subscription, deleting your account does not cancel it: cancel it in the app first, or in your Apple ID or Google Play subscriptions.

Need help?

If you can't get into the app, email us from the address on your account and we'll delete it for you within 30 days, usually within 48 hours.

Email: support@proxtrades.com
